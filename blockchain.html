🛡️ Hybrid Architecture: On-Chain vs Off-Chain Data Separation
PRIVACY PRESERVING

To comply with data privacy standards (GDPR & DPDP Act) and gas optimization, VarshaRakshak maintains strict separation between immutable public smart contracts and encrypted off-chain application storage.

⛓️

ON-CHAIN (Ethereum Sepolia Ledger)

  • ✓ Policy Terms Hashes: 256-bit Keccak hashes of immutable underwriting terms
  • ✓ Pseudonymous Rider IDs: One-way cryptographic hash of delivery partner IDs
  • ✓ Weather Anomaly Attestation: Oracle-submitted rainfall & temperature breach proofs
  • ✓ Claim Lifecycle State: Duplicate prevention map & immutable decision records
  • ✓ Payout Authorizations: Public settlement receipts verifiable on Etherscan
🔒

OFF-CHAIN (Encrypted Local Storage / DB)

  • ✗ Personal Identifying Information: Rider full names, phone numbers & contact info
  • ✗ Earnings Records: Daily gross wages, delivery trip counts, and platform tips
  • ✗ Real-time Telemetry: GPS breadcrumb coordinates, routes & delivery destinations
  • ✗ Vehicle Information: Registration plate numbers and license documents
  • ✗ Raw Weather Feeds: High-frequency radar streams and weather radar arrays

SHA-256 DOCUMENT SECURITY
